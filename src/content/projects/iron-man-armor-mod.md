## Status

Version 0.2.0. This stage builds the materials the armour will need; the suits themselves aren't in yet.

## What's in so far

- **Ores:** magnesium and titanium ore, with raw drops that smelt or blast into ingots.
- **Anvil forging:** iron with coal or charcoal makes a Steel Sheet, iron with copper makes an Iron-Copper Alloy, and that alloy with magnesium makes an Iron-Copper-Magnesium Alloy.
- **Arc reactor:** palladium, and a Palladium Core Arc Reactor crafted from palladium ringed with copper ingots and glass.
- Everything sits in its own Iron Man Armory creative tab, with block states, models, loot tables and recipes data-generated.
